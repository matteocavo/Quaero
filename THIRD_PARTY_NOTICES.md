## 1. Datasets

Quaero does not ship these datasets: the raw, staging, integrated and mart outputs generated by the
pipeline are git-ignored. They are downloaded when a pipeline is run, and any output derived from them
carries the upstream terms below.

### 1.1 World Bank - World Development Indicators

Used by `projects/global_economic_indicators/` (inflation and unemployment series, fetched from the
World Bank API at run time):

| Dataset | World Bank indicator | Underlying source named in the indicator metadata |
|---|---|---|
| Inflation, consumer prices (annual %) | `FP.CPI.TOTL.ZG` | International Financial Statistics database, International Monetary Fund (IMF) |
| Unemployment, total (% of total labor force) (modeled ILO estimate) | `SL.UEM.TOTL.ZS` | ILO Modelled Estimates database (ILOEST), International Labour Organization (ILO) |

- **Attribution:** The World Bank - World Development Indicators.
- **License:** the World Bank Group licenses its open datasets under
  [Creative Commons Attribution 4.0 International (CC BY 4.0)](https://creativecommons.org/licenses/by/4.0/)
  by default, and states that some datasets are available under other licenses and are labelled
  accordingly ([World Bank public licenses](https://datacatalog.worldbank.org/public-licenses)).
  Indicators that originate from other organizations (here the IMF and the ILO) may carry additional
  terms: check the indicator page before redistribution.
- **Changes made by Quaero (CC BY 4.0 requires indicating changes):** the pipeline filters the
  `World` aggregate, renames and standardizes columns, cleans values and aggregates them into marts.
- **World Bank values are not MIT.** They remain under the World Bank / CC BY 4.0 terms, including in
  any generated mart, integrated dataset, report or chart that contains them.

| Our World in Data | World CO2 emissions (`owid-co2-data`) | CC BY 4.0 for data produced by Our World in Data; third-party data made available by OWID keeps the original authors' terms ([repository](https://github.com/owid/co2-data)) | verified |
| NASA | Global mean sea level chart data from the NASA sea level page | NASA's general guidelines state that NASA content is generally not subject to copyright in the United States and that NASA should be acknowledged as the source ([NASA media usage guidelines](https://www.nasa.gov/nasa-brand-center/images-and-media/)); terms specific to this dataset were not retrievable | general guideline verified; dataset-specific terms **not verified** |
| NSIDC | Monthly Northern Hemisphere sea ice extent files | The applicable data-use and citation terms could not be retrieved; see [NSIDC](https://nsidc.org/) | **not verified** |
| World Bank | Indicator series (JSON API) | See section 1.1 | verified |

## 2. Code loaded at run time
